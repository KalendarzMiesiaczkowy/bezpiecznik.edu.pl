# Topics

Fixed list used in the `topics:` field of every chunk (slug, meaning). `tools/check_chunks.py` rejects any other slug.

- `numery-alarmowe` - emergency numbers (112, 997, 998, 999, ...) and how to make an emergency call
- `sygnaly-alarmowe` - alarm signals (sirens), warning channels (RSO, Alert RCB), meaning of the signals
- `schron-schronienie` - shelters and where to hide (sheltering rules)
- `ewakuacja` - evacuation: when and how, what to do before leaving
- `plecak-ewakuacyjny` - evacuation backpack contents and checklist
- `zapasy-72h` - home stock for at least 3 days
- `woda-jedzenie` - drinking water and food in the stock
- `brak-pradu` - long power outage (blackout)
- `powodz` - flood: before, during, after
- `pozar` - fire: how to react, what to extinguish with what
- `pierwsza-pomoc` - first aid basics (assessment, calling for help, ABCDE)
- `rko-aed` - cardiopulmonary resuscitation of adults and the AED
- `rko-dzieci` - resuscitation of children and infants
- `krwotok` - severe bleeding
- `zadlawienie` - choking
- `pozycja-boczna` - recovery (safe) position
- `oparzenia` - burns
- `utoniecie` - drowning
- `plan-rodzinny` - family crisis plan, meeting places, contacts
- `przygotowanie-domu` - preparing the home or flat (detectors, shut-offs, extinguisher)
- `przygotowanie-osobiste` - personal preparation (health, medicines, general rules)
- `osoby-szczegolnej-troski` - older people, people with disabilities, people needing special help
- `zwierzeta` - pets and farm animals
- `transport-samochod` - car and travel preparation
- `tlum` - safety in a crowd
- `zagrozenia-cbrn` - chemical, biological, radiological and nuclear contamination
- `zagrozenie-atak` - violent attack and terrorist threat
- `higiena` - hygiene without running water
